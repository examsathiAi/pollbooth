# PollBooth — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE OF LAUNCH]_

This Privacy Policy explains how PollBooth collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal
data, in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)
and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

## 1. What Data We Collect

| Data | Why we collect it | Required or optional |
|---|---|---|
| Phone number (stored as a secure hash, not plain text where possible) | To create your account and verify you via OTP | Required |
| Username | To identify you within the Platform | Set at signup |
| City, state | To show you regionally relevant polls and civic content | Optional (progressive) |
| Age bracket, gender, income bracket, education, employment | To improve poll targeting relevance and for aggregated research (never sold individually) | Optional (progressive) |
| Votes, opinions, reactions | To operate the core voting/opinion features | Necessarily collected as you use the Platform |
| Device/session information, IP address (for rate-limiting and abuse prevention) | Security and fraud prevention | Automatically collected |

We only collect demographic data (age, gender, income, etc.) with your explicit,
affirmative action to provide it — never pre-filled, never assumed, never required to use
core features. You can decline to answer any optional field.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- To operate the Platform: authenticate you, record your votes/opinions, show you
  relevant polls, calculate streaks and badges.
- To moderate content and enforce our Terms.
- To generate **aggregated, anonymized** statistics and research reports (e.g., "68% of
  respondents in Delhi support X") — these reports never contain information that
  identifies you individually, and are only shared with third parties where you have
  given specific, separate consent for this use, and only when the underlying data group
  (cohort) is large enough that no individual can be identified.
- To send you notifications about activity relevant to you (e.g., your opinion received
  agreement, a poll you follow is trending) — you can manage notification preferences in
  Settings.

## 3. What We Never Do

- We never sell your individual, identifiable data to any third party.
- We never share your phone number, real name, or exact location with other users.
- We never use your data for anything beyond what is described in this Policy without
  first asking for your consent.

## 4. Your Consent

At signup, you will be asked to explicitly consent to:
- Processing your phone number and account data to operate the Platform (required to use
  PollBooth).
- (Separately, optional) Use of your anonymized, aggregated activity data for research
  and analytics purposes.

You may withdraw optional consent at any time from Settings → Data Privacy. Withdrawing
consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal, and
does not require you to delete your account.

## 5. Data Retention

- **Server access and security logs are retained for a maximum of 180 days**, after which
  they are automatically and permanently deleted, unless a longer period is required to
  investigate a specific reported abuse or is required by law.
- Your account data (votes, opinions, profile information) is retained for as long as
  your account is active.
- If you delete your account, your personal data is deleted or irreversibly anonymized
  within 30 days, except where we are required to retain certain records for longer
  under applicable law (e.g., records relevant to an ongoing legal or regulatory matter).
- Guest (non-signed-up) votes cast via shared links are retained for a shorter period
  (30 days) before being purged, since no ongoing account relationship exists.

## 6. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erase** your data (subject to Section 5's exceptions).
- **Withdraw consent** for any optional processing (e.g., research data sharing).
- **Nominate** another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of
  your death or incapacity.
- **Grievance redressal** — see Section 8 below.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 8. We will
respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:
password/OTP-based authentication, hashed storage of sensitive identifiers where
technically feasible, access controls limiting who within our team can view user data,
and encrypted data transmission (HTTPS) for all Platform traffic.

No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are
committed to promptly investigating and addressing any data security incident, and to
notifying affected users and the relevant authority as required by law if a breach occurs.

## 9. Children's Data

PollBooth is not intended for use by anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from
children under this age. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data
from a user under 16, we will delete it promptly.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material
changes through the Platform before they take effect.
